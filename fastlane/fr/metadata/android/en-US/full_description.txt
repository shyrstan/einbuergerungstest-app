Prepare for the French civic exam (examen civique), required since 1 January 2026 for a first multi-year residence permit (CSP), a first resident card (CR) and naturalisation. The app contains every knowledge question from the official lists of the French Ministry of the Interior, with the correct answer and an explanation for each one, plus situational questions so you can practise the practical part.

WHAT YOU GET
• The official knowledge questions for your application: CSP (190), resident card (209) or naturalisation (258)
• 60 situational questions modelled on the practical questions of the exam
• An explanation for every question: why the answer is right, with references (Constitution, 1789 Declaration, laws)
• Translation of the question and answers as a reading aid – the exam is in French, so you learn the official wording
• Mock exam just like the real one: 40 questions (28 knowledge + 12 situational), 45 minutes, instant result
• Learn by the 5 official themes: principles and values of the Republic, institutions, rights and duties, history-geography-culture, living in French society
• Smart repetition: questions you got wrong come back more often
• Review your mistakes with one tap
• Statistics: how many questions you know and how your mock exams went
• Works offline, no account needed

HOW THE EXAM WORKS
It is a digital multiple-choice test in French: 40 questions, 4 options and only one correct answer each, 45 minutes at most. You need at least 32 correct answers (80%). Each application type (CSP, CR, naturalisation) has its own list of questions.

LANGUAGES
App interface and explanations: Français, English, العربية, Türkçe, Русский, Українська, Português, 中文.
