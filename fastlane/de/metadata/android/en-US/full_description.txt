Prepare for the German citizenship test (Einbürgerungstest / "Leben in Deutschland") with all 300 general questions and the 10 questions for your federal state from the current official question catalogue (as of 7 May 2025). Every question comes with a clear explanation – in German and, if you like, in English, Turkish, Arabic, Russian or Ukrainian.

WHAT YOU GET
• All 310 questions for your federal state – including every picture question (coats of arms, maps, symbols)
• An explanation for every question: why the answer is right, with references to the Basic Law and other laws
• Translation of the question and answers as a reading aid – the test itself is in German, so you learn the original wording
• Exam simulation just like the real test: 33 questions (30 general + 3 about your state), 60 minutes, instant result
• Learn by 15 topics: basic rights, elections, history, Europe, everyday life and more
• Smart repetition: questions you got wrong come back more often
• Review your mistakes with one tap
• Statistics: how many questions you know for sure and how your practice exams went
• Works offline, no account needed

HOW THE TEST WORKS
In the "Leben in Deutschland" test you get 33 questions and 60 minutes. For naturalisation you need at least 17 correct answers (15 are enough for the orientation course). All possible questions are published in advance – if you practise them, you are well prepared.

LANGUAGES
